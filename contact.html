VISITOR INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Practical details for planning your private salon consultation or flagship appointment.

Are walk-in visits accepted at the salons?

To ensure uninterrupted attention and personal fitting privacy, all STACKLY boutique suites operate strictly by prior appointment. Same-day appointments may be arranged subject to concierge availability.

Is there private parking or valet available?

Yes. All global flagships, including our Salem Executive Tower, Paris Place Vendôme, and New York Fifth Avenue salons, feature secured underground parking with dedicated valet and private elevator access.

Can I bring guests or my personal stylist?

We welcome your trusted companions, personal stylists, or bridal party members. Our fitting salons accommodate up to six guests comfortably with champagne and tea service.
