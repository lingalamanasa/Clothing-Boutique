HEIRLOOM CARE PROTOCOL

The Art of Textile Longevity

Noble natural fibers deserve thoughtful stewardship. Follow our atelier preservation rules to ensure your garments outlast generations.

💨

Steaming Over Ironing

Never press flat irons onto silk charmeuse or velvet. Use low-pressure vertical steam to let fibers relax naturally.

🌲

Red Cedar Archival Storage

Store cashmere coats and tuxedos with natural cedar blocks in breathable unbleached cotton garment bags.

🪢

Unlaced Corset Relaxation

Always loosen spiral-steel corset lacings completely before laying flat in temperature-controlled dressing wardrobes.

🏷️

Atelier Annual Re-Fresh

Bring your pieces to our Salem or Paris salons annually for complimentary seam inspection and hand-conditioning.
