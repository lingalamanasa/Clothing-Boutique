THE SARTORIAL PROCESS

The Four-Stage Bespoke Protocol

A time-honored discipline ensuring each custom garment delivers anatomical perfection without compromise.

01

Private Consultation

Exploration of event context, posture, and textile selection over vintage champagne in our private suite. 32 precise body measurements recorded.

02

Muslin Calico Toile

A mock garment crafted from raw unbleached cotton is draped onto your physique to sculpt the baseline balance, armhole depth, and seam flow.

03

Structural Basting Fit

The final silk or cashmere is hand-cut and loosely basted with white cotton thread. Spiral steel boning and collar heights are calibrated.

04

Final Gala Reveal

Hand-finished buttonholes, pressed on antique irons, and delivered in custom monogrammed garment travel chests with lifetime care warranty.

COMMON INQUIRIES

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our bespoke commissions, lead times, and private salons.

How much advance notice is required for a bespoke gown?

Standard bespoke commissions generally take 4 to 8 weeks, encompassing the initial toile fitting and hand-finishing. However, for critical red-carpet engagements, our 24-hour and 72-hour express atelier options are available.

Can I choose custom fabrics not shown in the showroom?

Yes. We maintain direct mill relationships with historic weavers in Como, Biella, and Lyon. If you require a custom dye lot, bespoke jacquard pattern, or specific lurex ratio, we can commission exclusive weaves.

Are fittings transferable between global cities?

Absolutely. You can conduct your muslin fitting at our Salem Executive Tower or New York salon, and schedule your final basting or delivery in Paris or Milan without delay.

What is included in the Traveling Tailor service?

Our senior couturier flies directly to your location with dedicated garment trunks, fabric swatches, and measuring instruments, offering full fitting sessions within your residence, suite, or yacht.
